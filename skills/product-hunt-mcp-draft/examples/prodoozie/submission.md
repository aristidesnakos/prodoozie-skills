## Description

> A Mac screen recorder for solo creators. Plan the video in sections, with AI if you like. Record each section as its own take,
> retake just the one that went wrong, and press stop: the silence before your first word and after your last is already cut.

## First comment

> Hi Product Hunt, I'm Ari, the maker of Prodoozie.
>
> Most screen recorders treat a video as one long take, so one mistake in minute four means recording it all again. Prodoozie treats
> a video as a list of sections, and each section keeps its own takes. Get section 3 wrong and you retake section 3. The rest stays
> where it was, and every take you replaced is kept.
>
> Around that:
> - **Plan:** outline the video as sections from your notes, a link or a PDF. AI helps if you add your own OpenRouter key. It only
>   plans; you choose what to retake.
> - **Stop:** the silence before your first word and after your last is trimmed, and long pauses are shortened, with Undo All.
>   Captions are written on your Mac, in English.
> - **Private text:** when you stop, it reads the take on your Mac and blurs names, emails, card numbers, keys and links it finds.
>   Save shows you every hidden stretch before the file is written. It can miss text, so look before you share.
> - **The look:** backgrounds, a camera bubble or side by side, cursor zoom, and several shapes in one save, up to 4K.
>
> It's a native Mac app for Apple Silicon on macOS 26. No account, and your recordings never leave your Mac.
>
> What would make you switch from the recorder you use now?
